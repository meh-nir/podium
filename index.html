    Calm your body

    ready

    Your body cannot panic and breathe out slowly at the same time. A long exhale is the fastest brake you have.

    Use it in the morning, right before you practise, and in the last five minutes before a real talk. Breathe in through your nose, out through your mouth.

    What fear gets wrong

    If you blank in the middle of a talk

    1. Stop. Pause. A three-second silence looks like thinking, not failing.
    2. Breathe out slowly once.
    3. Look at your note card for the next keyword.
    4. Say: "Let me go back to my main point." Then say your main point again.
    5. Or repeat the last thing you said in different words. Your brain will catch up.
    6. Nobody knows your script. If you skipped something, they will never notice.

    Before a real talk

    • Memorise your first two sentences word for word. The opening is when nerves peak; a fixed opening carries you through it.
    • Know your three main points, not the whole script. Speak from keywords on small cards.
    • Practise out loud at least three times. Practising in your head does not count.
    • Do one practice in front of a person, even one friend, even on a video call.
    • Arrive early. Stand where you will speak. Say one sentence out loud in the empty room.
    • Two minutes of long-exhale breathing right before you start.
    • Say your first sentence slower than feels natural. Nervous speakers rush.
    • Find three friendly faces in different parts of the room and talk to them.
    • Plan your last line. A clear ending ("Thank you. I'm happy to take questions.") feels much better than fading out.
    • Afterwards, write down one thing that went well before anything that went wrong.

    Speech structures

    Fear gets worse when you don't know what to say next. A structure tells you. Learn PREP first; it fits almost every question.

    Useful phrases

    Press Hear to listen to the phrase, then say it out loud three times. Learn whole phrases, not single words: they come out automatically when you are nervous.
